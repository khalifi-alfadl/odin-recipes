Egg Prata Recipe

avlxyz, CC BY-SA 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients:

The Dough

  • All-Purpose Flour
  • Water
  • Condensed Milk
  • Salt
  • Ghee or Melted Butter

The Filling and Cooking

  • Fresh Eggs
  • Extra Ghee for frying

Cooking Instructions:

Dough Prep

  1. Mix the flour, water, condensed MILK, salt, and ghee together in a bowl until a soft dough forms.
  2. Knead the dough until smooth, divide it into small balls, coat them in ghee, and let them rest covered for 2 hours.

Stretching and Adding Egg

  1. Oil your workspace and flip, stretch, or roll out a dough ball until it is paper-thin and translucent.
  2. Crack a fresh Egg onto the center of the stretched dough and spread it out slightly with a spoon.
  3. Fold the four edges of the dough inward to form a square envelope over the egg filling.

Pan Frying

  1. Heat a flat griddle or skillet over MEDIUM heat and grease it Generously with ghee.
  2. Place the folded prata onto the hot griddle and cook for 2 to 3 minutes until the bottom turns golden brown.
  3. Flip the prata over and cook the other side for another 2 minutes until crispy and completely cooked through.
  4. Remove from heat, clap the edges with your hands to fluff it up, and serve immediately.
