Steak Recipe

LuvsMG481, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

The Steak

  • Ribeye or New York Strip Steak
  • Salt
  • Black Pepper

Aromatics and Basting

  • Unsalted Butter
  • Garlic Cloves
  • Fresh Rosemary
  • Fresh Thyme

Cooking Instructions:

Preparation

  1. Remove the steak from the refrigerator 30 minutes before cooking to bring it to room temperature.
  2. Pat the steak completely dry with paper towels on all sides.
  3. Season generously with salt and black pepper just before cooking.

Searing and Basting

  1. Heat a heavy cast-iron skillet over high heat until smoking hot, then add a splash of high-heat oil.
  2. Place the steak in the skillet and sear for 2 minutes without moving it to form a dark crust.
  3. Flip the steak and add the butter, garlic cloves, rosemary, and thyme to the pan.
  4. Tilt the pan slightly and spoon the melted butter continuously over the top of the steak for another 2 minutes.

Resting

  1. Remove the steak from the pan and place it onto a cutting board.
  2. Let the steak rest for 5 to 10 minutes to allow the juices to redistribute before slicing. Enjoy!
